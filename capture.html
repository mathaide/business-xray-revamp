◉ Capture integrity — Mitra-marked geofence. The Mitra establishes the business on-site: all photos are live-captured (camera only, no gallery), in a single session, from inside the same establishment, and the Mitra marks the business's GPS boundary during the visit. Each frame is geotagged + timestamped and must fall within that marked boundary; frames out of sequence, or with device/EXIF/reuse anomalies are rejected and re-prompted. The street / neighbourhood shots are the intended exception — they are deliberately taken outside the boundary and graded for locality / catchment quality rather than rejected. The marked boundary is then independently verified later by the CPV agent — a downstream check, not the capture-time source.
