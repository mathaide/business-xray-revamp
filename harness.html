What the harness is

The harness wraps the model in eight cooperating components, each with one job, a typed contract and its own failure behaviour — so a fault is contained and observable, not silently corrupting the case. It acquires, estimates, reconciles and recommends across all 56 profiles; a human decides.

🧭Planner

Chooses the next objective by expected uncertainty reduction per unit cost/time (Eq-7).

⚡Executor

Runs one planned step via the Tool Router or the capture sub-agent. Deliberately thin.

🛡️Verifier

Independently checks every output — schema, business rules, guardrails — before it enters memory.

🧠Memory

Four tiers: working state, human-confirmed facts, append-only audit log, cross-case priors.

🔀Tool Router

The only path out. Allowlist, scoped short-lived creds the model never sees, retries, logging.

✋Approval Gate

Blocks human-required steps until a named human approves. Tiered by risk × impact.

📝Logger / Tracer

One trace_id per case, span_id per step — every prompt, tool call and decision.

🔗X-Ray Integrator

Typed adapter to the Business X-Ray engine: pulls diagnostics per profile, proposes new signals.

Governing objective. Minimise credit risk while keeping TAT under 48 hours, with full regulator-grade auditability and human-only sanction. Every stage below is justified against it — for every industry profile.

The 10-stage workflow

A short, mostly-fixed plan made adaptive: the loop chooses whether to acquire more evidence, ask, pull an external signal or stop — by what most reduces decision-relevant uncertainty, given the TAT budget. The plan is identical across profiles; the profile decides which evidence and floors bind.
